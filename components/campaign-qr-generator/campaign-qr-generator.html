🖨️ Print Physics & Marketing Operations Guidelines

1. Minimum Print Dimensions

• Business cards / Badges (0.5m scan): 20 × 20 mm
• Table tents & A5 flyers (1.0m scan): 35 × 35 mm
• Pull-up booth banners (2.5m scan): 100 × 100 mm
• Overhead backdrop signage: 250+ mm

2. Lighting & Finish Physics

• Always enforce dark modules on light background.
• Avoid heavy gloss laminate coatings which produce camera flash specular glare.
• Matte, uncoated, or silk cardstock provides maximum optical scannability.

3. Webflow 301 Deployment

• In Webflow Designer: Project Settings → Hosting → 301 Redirects.
• Paste the Old Path and Redirect To values from the rule box above.
• Publish the site to activate the vanity redirect immediately.
